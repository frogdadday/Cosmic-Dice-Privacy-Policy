Cosmic Dice

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 8, 2026

Free. Simple. Respectful. Cosmic Dice has no advertisements, subscriptions, paywalls, accounts, analytics, or tracking. Frogdadday does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information.

How the app works

Cosmic Dice generates dice rolls entirely on your device. Die selection, colors, Cosmic Mode, animations, and roll results are processed locally. The app does not send rolls or choices to Frogdadday or any developer-operated server.

Information and permissions

Cosmic Dice does not ask you to create an account or enter personal information. It does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, files, camera, microphone, advertising identifier, or notifications.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, profiling, or third-party tracking SDKs. Core dice rolling works without an internet connection.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Cosmic Dice does not maintain a persistent roll history or developer cloud storage. Current selections and results exist only as part of the app experience. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes app data controlled by Android. Frogdadday cannot view, recover, or remotely delete information on your device because the app does not transmit it to Frogdadday.

Optional support

The Support button opens Frogdadday's Buy Me a Coffee page in your browser or another capable app. Support is entirely optional, never unlocks features, and never changes app access. Cosmic Dice does not send roll data with that link or track whether you donate.

Buy Me a Coffee and your browser may process information under their own policies. If you choose to support the developer, payment processing and any information you provide are handled by that service. See Buy Me a Coffee's privacy policy.

This privacy webpage

The app's Privacy button opens this page in your browser. This page contains no added analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking scripts.

GitHub Pages hosts this webpage. Visiting it is separate from using Cosmic Dice, and GitHub may process connection and usage information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Cosmic Dice is a general-audience utility. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released. The effective date above will also be updated.
